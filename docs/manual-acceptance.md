# Manual acceptance

Follow this once before calling the work done.

1. `pnpm install`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm dev`. Open the printed local URL.
2. The landing page opens. **Open the study** (or Enter) opens the model. The footer affiliation sentence is visible. A one-time hint names the mouse controls. Press 2 then F for the -900 side view, orthographic, nose to the left, skin on.
3. The length string reads `66.80 m` with badge `sourced`. A visual frame, if structure is turned on, does not show a millimetre and does show `schematic`.
4. In **Layers**, switch on structure, fuel, landing gear, and engines together. Colors match the table. In the bottom dock, open **Explode** and scrub from 0 to 100% and back. At 0 the skin sits on the body. At 100% the skin is above it. **Reassemble** (or E) returns to 0.
5. Hover the Structure row and press its target icon to solo it. Other layers ghost and a banner says so. **Clear** in the banner ends the solo.
6. In the dock, open **Section** and pick X. A fuselage-station cut and the hatch appear. Switch the axis to Z.
7. **Side by side** (split vertical). Orbit one pane; the other follows. Open **Sync** and switch Camera off. Each pane gets its own view toolbar. Orbit again; the other pane stays. **Link all** links again.
8. **Overlay**. Wing boxes sit on top of each other. Noses do not. Amber marks the -1000. The spec panel becomes **Compare**, with the stretch and height delta graded `derived`. Turn on differences. Plugs and the extra gear wheels highlight. The Door 3 note is visible and does not claim a millimetre.
9. Hover the engine rating. The -900 shows the EASA thrust and a conflict badge whose alternate is 84,000 lbf. The plate is not a yellow edge. The -1000 shows 97,000 lbf without that conflict. With differences on, the -900 has yellow only on shifted nose, tail, fin, horizontal tail, nose gear, and cabin monuments.
10. Open **Limits**. The gap list includes frame stations, ply books, and hydraulic routes. Press ? for the controls sheet. Esc closes either one.
11. Narrow the window under 768 px. The layout switches to Model, Layers, Flight, and Data tabs. The split modes are gone. Explode and section sit in the Flight tab. Orbit still works.
12. `pnpm build` and `pnpm preview`. The preview URL loads the same default view. The browser network panel shows no requests to a third-party host on load.
